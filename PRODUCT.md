# Dashboard Icons

## Register

product

## Users

People configuring dashboards and app directories who need to find an icon, choose an available Variant and copy a usable URL.

## Product Purpose

Browse native and external icon collections with source attribution, available formats and direct asset links. The README defines the primary workflow: search, open details, choose a format, then download or copy its URL.

## Brand Personality

Practical, clear and community-maintained. Preserve the existing interface and shared design tokens.

## Anti-references

Invented brand descriptions, keyword-stuffed marketing copy and nested-card bloat are outside the external icon page brief.

## Design Principles

- Show useful information from actual catalogue data.
- Keep source identity and artwork licensing explicit.
- Reuse shared URL helpers, display names and components.
- Make discovery links available in server-rendered HTML.
- Keep the copy-and-download workflow usable on narrow screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The external icon page acceptance criteria require no clipping at 390×844 and 1440×900, touch targets of at least 44px and visible keyboard focus. Respect reduced-motion preferences and the existing light and dark themes.
